# Erdős Problem 634 — How many congruent triangles can tile a triangle?

[Erdős problem 634](https://www.erdosproblems.com/634) asks which integers $N$ can occur when a triangle is cut into $N$ congruent triangles. The pieces may be reflected, and one edge may meet several shorter edges.

[Project page with the 280-triangle dissection](https://denisukranian.github.io/erdos-634-tilings/).

**Rigid tiles and the next 5063 mode.** At 5063, a tiling with exactly 215 odd-height tiles must put all of them at height one; every short height is then 0, 1, or 2. A fresh exact enumeration excludes 9,112,001 profiles from the two other height alternatives. Balanced two-block nodes further reduce the remaining middle layer to one, two, or four Euclidean classes. At 3431, an exact fractional witness survives the full cylinder contact model even inside the convex hull of actual nonoverlapping 111-tile packings: the contact and charge conditions hold in the average, not in each packing. A physical charge-budget theorem states the integer global obstruction still required. **Both original counts remain open.** [Proofs, audits, and the precise role of integrality](research/2026-11-21/semiprimes/global-closure-next/README.md).

**A global charge and contact theorem.** The two prime trees can be coupled in one graph with an edge for each unmatched physical tile. Signed charges and enclosed areas must use the same contacts and fit one exact centroid budget; each active height band is connected. The theorem applies beyond the minimal odd mode. Its exact controls reconstruct two complete 143-tile tilings. A separate rational lifting result identifies a limit of first positional moments at 3431. The original counts 3431 and 5063 remain open. [Theorem, proof, checks, and remaining gap](research/2026-11-21/semiprimes/global-closure-next/README.md).

**What happens if you keep the tile?** Fix any triangular tile and let the outside triangle vary. Apart from finitely many exceptions, every possible count belongs to one of finitely many families $Ck^2$, and every member of those families occurs. The proof builds large triangles from two smaller tilings of the same shape. The remaining problem is to describe the counts when the tile can vary too. [Proof and example](https://github.com/DenisUkranian/erdos-634-tilings/blob/a837eb58e837132734342a53b889aade5cad723b/research/2026-11-19/structural/FIXED_SHAPES.md).

**Products of two primes.** Each prime above three has at most one possible partner in the mixed-residue case. For a concrete complete row, $1031q$ tiles exactly for $q=631,1009,1031,1607$ among primes. The original counts 3431 and 5063 remain open. Their boards have certified packings of 3427 and 5059 complete tiles, respectively. [Coordinates and geometric proofs](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/packing/README.md). For each one, attaching a single tiled parallelogram gives a convex trapezoid with an explicit tiling. Recovering the board requires a tiling that respects their entire common side. [Partner theorem and classifier](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/partners/README.md) · [Trapezoid construction](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/signed/PADDING.md) · [Earlier geometric results](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/README.md).

**The entire minimal odd mode at 5063 is excluded.** Every complete tiling of the specified 5063 board needs at least **215 odd-height tiles**. The new proof excludes all `n_odd=129` charge profiles, including charged remote branches: 65 exact endpoint certificates, 1,442,220 independently checked integer column inequalities, and a fresh exhaustive enumeration with no survivors. Larger odd populations and the original counts 3431 and 5063 remain open. [Full proof, certificates, independent audit and replay](research/2026-11-21/semiprimes/README.md).

**Unrestricted semiprime search.** A new endpoint-current method can check a proposed global obstruction by enumerating at most 24 placements per supported row, with arbitrary orientations and T-junctions included. An integer version completes selected rows and uses temporary assumptions only to guide expansion. Both have exact independent positive controls; neither has decided 3431 or 5063. A separate census records seven residual mixed F2 products through one million and explicitly separates the distant cases dependent on geometric manuscripts. [Proof, checkers, searches and precise status](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/global-endpoints/README.md).

**Stronger phase and corner restrictions.** Exterior-free ancestors of occupied phase nodes cannot be vacant on either target board. Separate direction-sign populations and height parity limit the total occupied ordinary nodes to 45 at 3431 and 58 at 5063; within one specified remote subtree the limits are 43 and 56. Complete local corner fans and shared height capacities strengthen the unrestricted search. The exact 143-tiling survives the combined solver and independent controls. Both target questions remain open; the two unguarded probes timed out without a decision. [Proofs, internal audits, finite replays and restartable checkpoints](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/vertex-phase/README.md).

**A common obstruction route for 3431 and 5063.** Joint prime-phase charges now require additional blocks of both signs, small exterior-free terminal nodes occupy one Euclidean translate, and every occupied exterior-free node has unmatched long sides. Exact bounds of 555 and 731 height-one tiles hold under explicitly stated native two-height hypotheses. Exact partial packings show why mixed-height cycle exclusion and automatic removal of terminal branches need additional global arguments. Neither count is decided. [Theorems, limits and exact controls](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/common-obstruction/README.md).

**Odd charge on actual contact boundaries.** Exact phase-cut identities now connect signed populations with real crossing long sides, including partial contacts and exterior terms. A balanced nonempty exterior-free cut still costs at least four boundary-carrying tiles; costs over distinct edges of one prime tree can be added without repetition. The smallest odd population is confined to a specific three-height configuration. An exact abstract axis-flow model shows why the current count and flow conditions still do not exceed either available budget. Both counts remain open. [Physical transport theorem, conditional geometry, and exact replays](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/charge-transport/README.md).

**Global geometric restrictions on the two remaining boards.** Every tiling either has an occupied exterior-free phase branch or uses three precisely specified Euclidean cells. Exact two-gate certificates prove that the entire latter class needs at least 185 odd tiles at 3431 and 215 at 5063. The full minimal `3c` mode is confined to 75 or 87 explicit cells. The initial 5063 certificate forced a charged even terminal branch; the newer result above excludes those charged profiles as well. A global cell inequality bounds the tiles needed at actual contact gates, and two ordered boundary quotients distinguish every prefix position on an upper median path. Seven exact suites pass; neither original count is decided. [Theorems, proofs, certificates and remaining cases](research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/global-closure/README.md).

**The 280-tile example.** A triangle with sides 90, 90 and 140 can be tiled by 280 copies of the triangle with sides 6, 5 and 9. This fills the last gap for the counts $70m^2$, which occur exactly when $m≥2$. [Picture, coordinates and both checks](https://github.com/DenisUkranian/erdos-634-tilings/blob/a837eb58e837132734342a53b889aade5cad723b/research/2026-11-19/positive280/README.md).
