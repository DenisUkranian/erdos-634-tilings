# Finite endpoint and point-packing certificates for the unrestricted 136 problem

The unit tile has sides $3,8,9$. In axial coordinates $x+i\sqrt{35}y$ the outer triangle has vertices $(0,0),(136,0),(68,4)$. This verifier considers arbitrary translations and arbitrary rotations of the physical tile.

Attach an integer weight $Y(p,h)$ to finitely many pairs of a position $p$ and an unoriented line direction $z^h$, where $z=(-1+i\sqrt{35})/6$. Attach a nonnegative integer weight $L(q)$ to finitely many rational test points $q$. Orient all triangles counterclockwise. Let $a(t)$ denote the sum of endpoint weights at the three starts of the edges of $t$, minus the weights at their three ends, using the line direction of that edge. Let $l(t)$ be the sum of $L(q)$ over points strictly inside $t$.

Write $B=a(\mathrm{outer})$ and fix the positive integer mass weight $k$. For a tiling with $n$ tiles the inequalities
$$
 B\ \le\ kn+\sum_q L(q)+\sum_t \max(0,a(t)-l(t)-k)
$$
hold, where the last sum runs over individually possible, pairwise distinct placements. Indeed oriented endpoint weights cancel along internal lines, at most one tile can contain a test point in its interior, and no tiling may include the same geometric placement twice.

All nonzero summands in the last sum can be enumerated exactly, even though the space of translations and rotations of a triangle is infinite. A tile with $a(t)>0$ must have at least one weighted endpoint at one of its edge directions. After fixing that endpoint and the line one chooses the side on that line, its two possible directions, the other incident side and the chirality. This supplies at most 24 possibilities. Triangles are deduplicated after sorting their vertices. Their coordinates are rational in the axial chart. The verifier tests containment exactly. If a vertex lies on an outer side it must be an end of a chain of whole boundary tiles, so its distances to both ends of that side must belong to the nonnegative semigroup generated by $3,8,9$. No column elimination or approximation from the numerical optimization is used by the verifier.

The attached gzipped JSON stores exact integers. Running the standard-library verifier prints the total target demand, the point-packing allowance, the sum of positive residuals, and the resulting rational lower bound. The certified numbers are target demand 699,192,857, packing allowance 560,713,722, and positive residual capacity 2,329,008 at mass weight 1,000,000. The resulting lower bound is $136.150127$, hence at least **137** triangles. Area independently requires exactly 136, so this target has no such tiling. The verification evaluated 232,806 distinct placements hitting 27,941 nonzero endpoint weights. It includes a second generation of each triangle template by the cosine law, independent of the angle parametrization. No prior upper bound on the number of orientations and no bounded coordinate lattice hypothesis is used. The discovery calculation selected weights using numerical optimization. Only the independently recomputed integer totals have evidential force.
