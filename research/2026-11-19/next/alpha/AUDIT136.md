# Independent replay of the no-clearance mesh and its composition with the exact lattice certificate

For the concrete target $(72,72,136)$ and tile $(3,8,9)$, the two computations together exclude a tiling. This is the scale-one test identified in [Beeson, §14.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1206.2229v6#S14.SS3). A different shape already realizes the numerical count 136.

**Direction chart.** With $d=i\sqrt{35}$, $w=(1+d)/2$ and $z=(w-1)/3$, the argument of $z$ is $\gamma$. A tile whose length-eight edge has undirected height $h$ has its other edge heights $h+\varepsilon$ (length 3) and $h-2\varepsilon$ (length 9). In the mesh code the anchor is an endpoint of the length-eight edge. Its vector is $8v$ with $v=\pm z^h$. The other vertex is $3zv$ or $-9z^2v$. These are precisely the two counterclockwise templates for that signed eight-edge. The outer vertices are $0,136,68+4d=64+8w$. The exterior rays have heights 0 and $\pm2$.

**Why freezing a 16-level buffer suffices.** At an interior point suppose there are $p,q,r$ tile angles $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$, and $t$ flat contributions $\pi$ from tile edges passing through the point. Irrationality of $\gamma/\pi$ and the angle identities give $q+r+2t=4$ and $2p+r=3q$. The changes in ray exponents when traversing an occupied sector are $2,-3,1,0$, respectively. The total of positive changes and the absolute total of negative changes are each $3q\le12$. The difference between exponents of any two incident rays is therefore at most 12. The analogous calculation at a straight boundary joint uses $q+r+2t=2$. At a base corner the fan has only one $\alpha$, and at the apex it has one $\alpha$ and two $\beta$. Along the interior portion of each of these boundary fans the exponent spread is at most 6. Every tile incident to a genuine fan has its eight-edge height within two steps of either of its rays. Hence a tile with $|h|\le28$ can have every supporting star witnessed among heights $[-44,44]$. For a positive-length shared edge, adjacent tile heights differ by at most four.

The outer levels $29\le|h|\le44$ retain all their feasible initial anchor cells throughout all passes; neither star pruning nor half-edge pruning updates them. Thus the preservation-by-induction proof of the central cells applies even if arbitrary orientations outside the computed universe exist. At each true vertex with a tile edge crossing through it, its distance to either endpoint is an integer, from the two complete banks of the straight seam. This explains the mesh's integer-offset semicircles. The midpoint of each unit subdivision of a true tile edge is matched on the opposite bank, unless the edge is on the target boundary. The directed half-edge test enumerates every such possibility. The rounded translations of each coordinate permit both its floor and its ceiling. These operations can add spurious witnesses but preserve every physical tiling. The full proof of the generic sector test is in the parallel project report; these checks specialize its hypotheses to $(3,8,9)$.

**Independent run.** I inspected the shape and wall definitions, permitted directed arcs, circular reachability, rounding, frozen-level skip conditions in both passes, and opposite directed sides. I then rebuilt the mesh implementation in arbitrary-precision mode and replayed it **with all positive-distance clearances disabled**. It stabilizes after 28 sweeps, with 29,659,440 occupied shape/cell pairs. Among heights $[-15,15]$ only $-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3$ survive. Remote levels, including $\pm16$, can still occur in this relaxation. A tile touching an outer base edge starts at $|h|\le2$, and every actual tile is reachable by positive-length shared edges. No such path with steps of at most four can leave $[-3,3]$ through the absent heights $4,5,6,7$ or $-4,-5,-6,-7$. Every tile of an actual tiling must therefore have $|h|\le3$. This argument requires no a priori bound on the full orientation range.

**Independent exact final inequality.** The whole-side connectivity argument of [GRID136.md](GRID136.md) now puts every vertex in $3^{-3}\mathbb Z[w]$. The denominator-27 pool leaves 13,536 candidate placements. The independently implemented integer verifier gives a target endpoint weight of 3,001 and a total positive capacity of 1,363 across all those placements. Their strict gap of 1,638 proves the claimed contradiction. The conclusion concerns this specific triangle and unit tile. It does not settle the other primitive shapes or scales in the general alpha conjecture.
