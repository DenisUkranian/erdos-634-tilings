# Two computer-assisted exclusions of the scale-one example in §14.3

A triangle with sides 72, 72, 136 cannot be dissected into congruent triangles with sides 3, 8, 9. In coordinates with axial basis \(1,i\sqrt{35}\), take the vertices \(0,136,68+4i\sqrt{35}\). The axial determinant of the target is 544 and that of each tile is 4, so a tiling would use exactly 136 tiles. The numerical Erdős count 136 nevertheless occurs by the classical construction since \(136=10^2+6^2\). [Beeson, §14.3, row 136](https://arxiv.org/html/1206.2229v6#S14.SS3).

## Proof by endpoint weights

Give each of finitely many point-and-undirected-line-direction pairs an integer weight. Traverse a polygon counterclockwise and charge each directed side its weight at the start minus its weight at the end, in that side's direction. Charges add when a segment is subdivided, and cancel along an internal boundary. Consequently the charge of any tiled target is the sum of the tile charges, also at T-junctions.

A second list assigns nonnegative weights to 291 packing points. The sum of the weights of the points strictly inside distinct tiles is at most their total \(P\). For a placement \(U\) let \(R(U)\) be the positive part of its boundary charge minus \(10^6\) minus the weights strictly inside \(U\). Every tile with \(R(U)>0\) has a weighted endpoint on a side in its assigned direction. For each such pair there are finitely many placements: fix the edge length, its sign, the adjacent side length and the half-plane containing the third vertex. This covers arbitrary real translations and rotations. A boundary vertex must be a joint on the complete outer side, so its distance to either end lies in the additive semigroup generated by 3, 8 and 9. No interior lattice assumption is used.

The certificate lists 27,941 nonzero endpoint weights. The two verifiers regenerate 232,806 distinct possible placements meeting at least one weight and the wall conditions. For target charge \(D=699192857\), total packing weight \(P=560713722\) and sum \(E=2329008\) of all positive remainders, every tiling into \(k\) unit tiles would satisfy
\[
10^6 k\ \ge D-P-E=136150127>136\cdot10^6.
\]
This contradicts the area ratio. The second verifier builds all edge normals directly from rational coordinates, rotates only the charged side, uses the cosine law for all six length assignments, and recomputes the two wall offsets from their physical lengths. A linear programming optimizer is not needed to check the certificate. [Weights and original checker](../alpha/ENDPOINT_LP136.md) · [Independent checker](recheck_direct.py) · [Output](direct-recheck.txt).

~~~sh

python3 ../alpha/verify_unrestricted136.py
~~~

## Proof by vertex stars and an exact denominator-27 certificate

There is also a proof using different constraints. Write \(z=(-1+i\sqrt{35})/6\). The length-eight side of any tile is parallel to \(z^h\) for some integer \(h\). Adjoining tiles across a positive-length contact changes this height by at most four. At a vertex, the angles \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\pi\) change the unoriented ray exponent by \(2,-3,1,0\), respectively. A complete star has at most four \(\beta\)'s, and the positive and negative changes each total at most 12. A tile's eight-side sits within two exponents of each of its rays. The exact cell test therefore checks all possible witnesses for any central height \(|h|\le28\) using a window of radius 44, provided all feasible cells in the outer sixteen layers are permanently retained. The base corners have angle \(\alpha\) and the apex has \(\alpha+2\beta\). The proof of the star rule, rounding, and integer offsets at through-edges is in [the mesh derivation](../beta/MESH_PROOF.md) and [the independent specialization](../alpha/AUDIT136.md).

Even the weaker test with no clearance and no edge-midpoint tests suffices. On mesh \(Q=36\) its initial 67,250,515 candidate shape/cell pairs fall to 29,720,527 at the fixed point reached on pass 46. All heights of both signs from 4 to 15 disappear. The base has a tile with \(|h|\le2\); all tile interiors are connected by a chain of positive-length edge contacts. No chain starting there can reach \(|h|\ge4\). The choice of finite central band places no prior restriction on the allowed orientation of a tile.

It follows that every whole-side vector belongs to \(3^{-3}\mathbb Z[(1+i\sqrt{35})/2]\). The length-three and length-nine vectors introduce at most one or two extra powers of three from \(z^{\pm1}\) and \(z^{\pm2}\), absorbed by the lengths 3 and 9. The complete banks of collinear sides connect all vertices to the origin using these vectors, even in a dissection with T-junctions. The denominator-27 enumeration retains 13,536 candidate placements. A separate integer endpoint certificate on that pool has target demand 3,001 and positive capacity 1,363, giving another contradiction. [Pool and exact weights](../alpha/GRID136.md).
