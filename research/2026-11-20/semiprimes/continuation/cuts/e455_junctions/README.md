# The equilateral 455-tile candidate has no straight fan partition

For the tile of sides 35, 13 and 43, let E be the equilateral triangle of side 455. Its area is 455 tile areas. Four copies of E and three ordinary triangular grids of scales 35, 13 and 43 would give the full 5063 board, since 4·455 + 35² + 13² + 43² = 5063. The project already excludes E itself via the [manuscript stopped-run theorem](../../counting/inputs/F2_V1_PURITY.md). The computation below independently excludes the listed straight partitions without using that theorem. The original 5063 board remains undecided.

**Theorem.** E has no partition into two or three convex polygons individually tileable by the specified tile. More generally, it has no proper partition into convex tileable sectors by straight arms from one interior point to the boundary. Each cell may use arbitrary rotations, reflections and T-junctions.

Research record, 20 November 2026. Prepared with AI assistance for the Denis Paliy project. The two implementations used for internal cross-checking use the project's direction and boundary identities. Independent refereeing remains outstanding.

## Finite list of partitions

Write ρ = exp(iπ/3), z = (35+13ρ)/43. The standard height convention writes a directed side as (l,h,j) with vector l zʰρʲ. The tile angles α opposite 35 and β opposite 13 obey α+β=π/3 and are rationally independent. An angle mα+nβ has direction increment (n−m,m). The third tile angle is 2α+2β. The established derivations are in [the cut theorem](../README.md) and [the whole-board direction note](../../../FULL_BOARD.md).

In a convex cell each bank is a concatenation of full tile sides, so all lengths belong to the semigroup generated by 35, 13 and 43. At an arm endpoint strictly inside an equilateral side, the two sectors split the coefficient pair (3,3), at an outer corner they split (1,1), and at an interior junction the sum of the sectors is (6,6). The signed weights of a directed side are l(−1)ʲ and l(−1)ʲ⁺ʰ. On individual tiles their absolute values are 21 and 65. Each boundary total, divided by the corresponding signed unit, is an integer of the same parity as the tile count and is bounded by that count in modulus.

If two tileable cells meet along a chord, their areas and boundary totals pass these tests. Taking endpoints (x,0) and (0,y) up to the equilateral symmetries enumerates every chord. None passes. Without such a chord, three convex cells have one interior junction with three arms ending on the exterior. Two convex cells can share at most one line segment. If three share two distinct points, the two banks of the intervening segment cannot accommodate three positive-area convex cells. Thus there is at most one common interior point, and every interior end of an interface must be that point.

The C++ enumerator lists integral exterior offsets, directions, and arm lengths. It groups the arms by their exact common endpoint, imposes convexity and the area and character tests, and emits 42 layouts. The Python checker reconstructs the cells and groups congruent triples. For each cell it enumerates compatible entire corner fans, including containment of every fan tile. On each side it tries full boundary words. The tile incident to an edge is fixed by the edge length and its two endpoint angles. Its apex must be inside the cell. At a join the endpoint angles used cannot exceed (3,3) coefficientwise. A cyclic dynamic program joins the six possible directed boundary-edge types to the fan choices at both ends. These are necessary conditions even if additional tiles meet the bank at a single vertex.

## Why the remaining quadrilaterals cannot tile

Write nₕ for the unsigned population at short height h, Gₕ for its positive minus negative population. Choose the sign so the contribution of an a-edge to the ordinary character is 35 times the tile sign. The ordinary and alternating weights of an entire tile are then −21 times its sign and 65 times its sign times (−1)ʰ, respectively. In particular nₕ and Gₕ have the same parity.

Both quadrilaterals have boundary heights −1 and 0. At every other height any positive nₕ is at least 86. Indeed, the threshold boundary identity expresses the boundary of the tail away from these exterior heights by full long edges. At each threshold k their vectors belong to the lattice Λₖ = 43 zᵏ (R+zR) for R = ℤ[ρ]. This lattice has determinant 43 in axial coordinates since z = (6+ρ)/(7−ρ) and the numerator and denominator are coprime of norm 43. A closed walk of such edges, based anywhere, has integral doubled axial area divisible by 43. Subtraction gives 43 | nₕ at exterior-free heights, using gcd(455,43)=1. The [line-count theorem](../../../LINE_COUNTS.md) excludes nₕ=43 at those heights.

At threshold −1, the exterior high arc is the pair of sides of height 0. For a set of tiles of short height greater than −1, its oriented boundary minus this arc consists of signed whole long edges of heights −1 and 0. To see the identity, sum tile boundaries, and also sum just the components of height greater than −1. Their difference on a tile can involve only its long edge. Close the exterior arc by the straight chord between its endpoints. The chord displacement belongs to Λ₋₁, as checked directly. The difference from the original closing chain is a sum of closed walks with steps in Λ₋₁, so the doubled axial area of the capped arc divided by 455 modulo 43 gives the population above −1 modulo 43. Here division means multiplication by the inverse of 455 modulo 43. Heights strictly above 0 or strictly below −1 have zero population modulo 43.

* For the 65-cell the successive edges are (52,0,0), (143,−1,1), (117,−1,2), (247,0,4). This gives n₋₁ ≡ 38 and n₀ ≡ 27 modulo 43. The two least nonnegative residues already sum to 65, so they are the exact counts. The characters give G₋₁ = −9 and G₀ = −4. The parity at height −1 is impossible.
* For the 91-cell the edges are (39,0,0), (221,−1,1), (130,−1,2), (325,0,4). Here n₋₁ ≡ 14 and n₀ ≡ 34 modulo 43. Including any further occupied height would require at least 14+34+86 > 91 tiles. The characters give G₋₁ = −10 and G₀ = −3. Parity then forces (n₋₁,n₀) = (14,77). Exactly two height −1 tiles are positive. The exterior edge (221,−1,1) must contain at least five 13-edges: the possible inventories (number of 35-, 13-, 43-edges) are (0,17,0), (1,11,1), (2,5,2). Each 13-edge in this bank accounts for a distinct positive tile at height −1 since its sign is opposite to (−1)ʲ. This is impossible.

Finally consider any number of rays from a single interior point giving convex tileable sectors. An opposite pair supplies a chord. With no opposite pair, the ray directions surround the origin and some three surround it strictly, by planar Carathéodory. Group the sectors between those rays. Each grouped sector is the intersection of E with a convex wedge, yielding an excluded three-cell partition. This also describes a property of unrestricted tilings: every interior point from which tile-edge segments reach the boundary sees all such boundary points along directions in one open semicircle, if the set of these directions is nonempty.

## Replay

Run `python3 verify.py` with Python 3.10 or later and a C++17 compiler. The checker takes several seconds and uses integer and rational arithmetic throughout. It checks all chords and stars, all seven boundary cases and both residue calculations. For a separate integer implementation of the underlying cut filters see the project's [straight-cut checker](../verify.py).
