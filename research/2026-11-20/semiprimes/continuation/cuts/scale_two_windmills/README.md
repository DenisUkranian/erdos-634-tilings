# Three-line partitions of the scale-two equilateral caps

The unit boards for 3431 and 5063 have sufficient equilateral caps of side 462 or 910 and area 924 or 1820 tiles, respectively. Here a windmill means a central triangle strictly inside the cap, together with three convex cells touching the exterior. Three lines carry the central sides and the three separating arms. Each line reaches a distinct boundary anchor beyond a vertex of the central triangle. Each outer cell has a boundary arc between successive anchors and two straight sides running to its interior corner. All four cells must be individually tileable. Their interior tilings may use any partial edge contacts and orientations.

| Cap side | Distinct multisets of four cell shapes after necessary arithmetic tests | Left by the manuscript stopped-run condition |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 462 | 35 | 0 |
| 910 | 137 | 3 |

Every remaining side-910 partition requires the same 480-tile quadrilateral. In physical axial coordinates in its own rotated frame its vertices are `(0,0), (48,-13), (468,-13), (0,455)`. The other non-elementary cell has area 591, 656 or 719. The remaining two cells in each case are a griddable similar triangle and a parallelogram spanned by integer multiples of the 35- and 13-edges at a tile-compatible angle. In the first case their areas are 1 and 748. On the original unit boards the same search produces 111 multisets for 3431 and 200 for 5063. The stopped-run filter leaves 18 and 67, respectively. The surviving [3431 cell lists](board_3431.json) and [5063 cell lists](board_5063.json) record areas and the ordered (length, height, phase) triples up to rigid motion. These counts allow the full-sized boards to be studied without prescribing either equilateral cap.

The stopped-run argument used for the exclusions is the project's [120-degree version with both acute labels](../../counting/inputs/F2_V1_PURITY.md). The finite list before that exclusion is independent of it. An [independent exhaustive local proof](../../packing/quadrilateral345/LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md) now eliminates the shared 480-cell. Consequently neither cap admits this windmill scheme. The unrestricted cap and full-board problems remain.

## Why the enumeration includes the specified layouts

Write rho = exp(i pi/3), z=(a+b rho)/c and represent an oriented edge by its length, its exponent h of z, and its exponent j of rho. The independent acute angles are alpha = pi/3-beta and beta=arg(z). At an anchor, a separating ray subdivides the outer angle into sums of these angles (with gamma=2alpha+2beta). The angle at a regular point of an exterior side is 3alpha+3beta. The C++ enumerator tries every such ray and every anchor at an integral distance for which both portions are sums of unit side lengths. From the anchor it tries every positive distance in the length semigroup, up to the opposite boundary. A side of an individually tileable convex cell between corners consists of whole exterior edges of the tiles. This also applies to each long side of an outer cell even when one central vertex lies on its open segment.

Joining two rays at an interior point gives a candidate outer cell. It must have positive integral tile area. Its ordinary and alternating signed perimeter sums, divided respectively by a-b-c and a-b+c, must be integers with the parity of its area in tile units and absolute value at most that area. These tests follow by cancelling interior sides in the two edge characters sending a directed edge of length s to s(-1)^j or s(-1)^(h+j). Each unit tile contributes either sign of the respective factor. The bounds permit non-edge-to-edge tilings. The enumerator uses integer coordinates with a common power of c as denominator.

Make a directed graph whose nodes are pairs consisting of an exterior anchor and the ray through it. The arc from one node to another records their common interior corner and the two ray lengths. A windmill gives a directed cycle of length three in counterclockwise exterior order. On each common ray the difference of the two lengths is the directed side of the central triangle. The Python verifier checks the three sides, the area of that triangle, angular feasibility and equality of the sum of the four areas to the cap area. It groups congruent cell shapes. A partition whose cells tile must survive these tests. The final filter excludes a cell when one of its supporting sides cannot contain the required number of c-edges according to the manuscript stopped-run argument. It requires gamma-free endpoint sectors, at most one designated acute angle at either endpoint, and both adjacent true sides longer than the purity bound times the starting-wall length. Thus the short side of the 480-cell prevents this filter from settling it.

Run `python3 replay.py` with Python 3.10 or later and a C++17 compiler. Use `python3 replay.py --boards` to include the original triangles. The replay also finds the gridded windmill on the 16-tile, fourfold-similar control triangle. The result concerns the stated four-cell architecture of the sufficient caps, not the unrestricted decision for either board.

## Independent direct check of all the cap eliminations

The local checker removes the stopped-run manuscript dependency from the two cap conclusions. For the side-462 cap eight selected convex cells cover every one of the 35 multisets. For side 910 twenty-one cells cover 134 of the 137 multisets; the other three all contain the separately excluded 480-cell. The main `replay.py` checks coverage by congruence of the full ordered polygons. The numerical row indices accompanying the cell list are diagnostic only. The local program directly checks every possible stopped block on one genuine supporting side of each chosen polygon, using its true vertices. At a side with gamma-free ends at least one doubly stopped block of long edges must occur; see the [counting and geometric inferences](../../packing/quadrilateral345/LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md). Zero long-edge capacity is therefore already contradictory. Unknown branches cause the verification to fail.

Run `python3 local/cases.py`. The 29 runs checked 8,876 physical starting cases for the 3431 cap and 259,476 for the 5063 cap. None remained unresolved. To complete the 5063 cap proof also replay the [12,356 cases for the 480-cell](../../packing/quadrilateral345/LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md). Along with the four-cell shape census this proves the two cap windmill exclusions without invoking the manuscript stopped-run adaptation. The 18 and 67 residual classes of windmills on the original triangles are separate from the two cap conclusions.
