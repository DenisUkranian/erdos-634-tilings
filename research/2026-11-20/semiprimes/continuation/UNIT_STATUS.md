# Status of the unit boards for 3431 and 5063

The unrestricted answers for both counts are open in this record. For 3431 the tile has sides 7, 33, 37 and the outer triangle has sides 511, 1369, 1551. For 5063 the respective sides are 13, 35, 43 and 1079, 1849, 2135. Use the [semiprime reduction](../README.md) for the hypotheses relating these boards to the original existential questions. A positive result must include exact full coordinates or a complete construction. A negative result for either number requires an unrestricted proof on its unit board and an audit of the classification reduction.

## Proven results and constructive reductions

* **5063, area 224.** The specific remaining convex quadrilateral in the certified packing cannot tile. Six possible populations reduce to 215 finite lattice configurations; every configuration fails at a required corner. Do not search the 224 cell again as a possible filling. [Exact proof and replay](packing/convex224/exclusion/PROOF.md) · [Original packing](packing/convex224/README.md).
* **5063, area 345.** The concave quadrilaterals in the larger symmetric repair have a supporting side of length 260 with at most three long edges. An independent physical search of all 812 possible stopped runs refutes them all. [Proof, geometry checks and 4373-tile coordinate certificate](packing/quadrilateral345/QUADRILATERAL_345.md).
* **5063, area 245.** The two-row pentagon has no tiling. Congruence and area restrict every tiling to three orientation intervals. Complete searches in all three return NO, with a successful positive control. [Proof and replay](packing/pentagon245/RESULT.md).
* **5063, area 312.** A larger pentagon is also excluded by four exhaustive orientation intervals. All 128 common convex repair cavities in the stated search through area 300 have a tiled complement in this pentagon. [Proof, data and independent nesting replay](packing/pentagon312/RESULT.md).
* **3431, area 1063.** There is a new sufficient pentagon with 226 retained-placement conditions. Its verifier lifts any valid filling to the original board. [Problem, coordinates and checker](packing/paired1063/README.md).
* **Both full boards.** For every chosen direction some tile without any positive-length exterior contact must have no edge in that direction. At least 31 tiles on the 3431 board and at least 45 on the 5063 board miss the proposed direction. [Proof and scripts](common-axis/PROOF.md).
* **Both full boards.** A hypothetical tiling must add at least 78 placements relative to the 3427 reference packing, or at least 86 relative to the 5059 packing. These lower bounds include arbitrary orientations and junctions. [Two-board replay](packing-distance-extended/README.md) · [stronger 3431 certificate](packing/paired1063/README.md).
* **All primitive F2 parameters.** The outer boundary word equals the identity in the universal group generated by directed unit edges modulo all tile relators. It also equals the identity in the position cover that permits every plane translate. Any obstruction through such a group must exploit board containment or positivity. [General algebraic proof](group/DIRECTION_GROUP.md). Moreover, existence is exactly equivalent to the least number of faces in a disc diagram being N. The current 5063 construction needs eight negative faces, giving nine possible values for the minimum area, from 5063 to 5079 in steps of two. [Exact whole-board minimum-area criterion](group/AREA_CRITERION.md).
* **Scale-two equilateral caps.** An exhaustive three-line windmill search gives 35 arithmetic candidates at side 462 and 137 at side 910 before a previous geometric restriction. It removes all 35 at side 462 and leaves three at side 910. They share the same 480-tile quadrilateral, [independently excluded](packing/quadrilateral345/LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md) by 12,356 physical run cases. A direct check of another 268,352 physical cases independently verifies the other cap eliminations. Thus this windmill scheme cannot fill either cap. The direct sweep on the full boards also leaves 18 or 67 candidate four-cell shape multisets, respectively. [Shapes and verifier](cuts/scale_two_windmills/README.md).

The side-455 equilateral has now also been [independently excluded](packing/quadrilateral345/LOCAL_OBSTRUCTION.md) in 12,356 local cases. The project previously excluded it by its [manuscript stopped-run argument](counting/inputs/F2_V1_PURITY.md). The [separate straight fan check](cuts/e455_junctions/README.md) does not depend on that argument. The side-910 cap is a different shape. These auxiliary exclusions do not imply that either original board is impossible.

* **Both full boards, signed discs.** The least number of negative faces is at most 75 for 3431 and at most eight for 5063. The 3431 bound improves the earlier 128 by allowing a ten-tile exterior padding disc. [Constructions and checker](group/diagram-bounds/README.md).
* **Convex sufficient targets.** A tiling of the specified 739-tile convex pentagon meeting 201 pair-retention conditions would complete the 3431 board. The analogous 1607-tile pentagon has 521 conditions and would complete 5063. The coordinate and lifting certificate is in [the repair archive](packing/paired-convex/convex_repairs.zip). A separate [enumeration and checker](packing/paired-convex/grid_census.zip) exclude all one-grid and two-grid straight-seam fillings of 2,380 and 11,095 convex repairs in the stated translated-pair construction.
* **Polygonal centers in the equilateral caps.** The cyclic layout exclusion now permits any number of arms and a simple central polygon with reflex corners. The complete enumeration finds 75 and 577 distinct shape multisets on the side-462 and side-910 caps. Every one contains a locally excluded cell. [Proof and replay](cuts/scale_two_windmills/rings/RINGS.md).
* **Four-cell schemes on the original boards.** Ordered boundary words eliminate 22 of the 67 previously surviving 5063 layouts. The other 45 and all 18 at 3431 pass this particular test. [Exact words and replay](cuts/scale_two_windmills/words/WINDMILLS.md).
