# Status of the unit boards for 3431 and 5063

The unrestricted answers for both counts are open in this record. For 3431 the tile has sides 7, 33, 37 and the outer triangle has sides 511, 1369, 1551. For 5063 the respective sides are 13, 35, 43 and 1079, 1849, 2135. Use the [semiprime reduction](../README.md) for the hypotheses relating these boards to the original existential questions. A negative result for either number requires an unrestricted proof on its unit board and an audit of the classification reduction.
