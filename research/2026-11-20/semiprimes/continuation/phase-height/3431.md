# Twenty placements are individually forbidden on the 3431 board

This strengthens the [direction-count result](../packing/BRIDGES.md). All twenty high placements from its 3427 packing are individually impossible in *every* complete 3431 tiling. The conclusion at height 41 uses the **position** of the tile in addition to its direction. The same position test works for other proposed placements on the whole board.

Use the notation of [the phase theorem](PHASE_BRANCHES.md) with $a=33,b=7,c=37$. Choose $\pi$ above 37 where $\rho=27\bmod\pi$; this is a root of $X^2-X+1$ and $33+7\rho=0\bmod\pi$. The other prime is distinct and does not divide $33+7\rho$. Since the norm of $33+7\rho$ is $37^2$ and 37 splits, $v_\pi(37)=v_\pi(z)=1$. The reference anchors in the [packing certificate](../packing/pentagon14.json) have the form $(X+Y\rho)/37^5$. Every one satisfies $X+27Y\not\equiv0\pmod{37}$, so its valuation is exactly $-5$. The second pentagon differs by the integral translation $-126+165\rho$.

**A uniform position exclusion on this board.** Every tile in a complete tiling with $v_\pi(x)\le-5$ at its anchor has short height at most **40**. To see this, suppose $h\ge41$. The ancestors at levels $-4,\ldots,41$ are in the exterior-free subtree rooted at level $-4$ through the anchor. At most one of these 46 nodes is empty, by the phase theorem. Each of the remaining nodes needs at least 74 tiles, even at height 0 since its node is remote. The subtree therefore takes at least $45\cdot74=3330$ of the 3431 tiles. At most 101 tiles are outside it. Every outer-edge tile lies outside it: its short and long nodes lie on neighboring levels, and each of the board-side heights 0 and 2 is above the unique cut between levels $-5,-4$.

Each of the three board sides is covered at least once by a tile long side. Indeed, if that side were partitioned into $j$ short tile sides, each would put its obtuse corner at one of the $j-1$ inner joins (the board corners are strictly less than 120 degrees). Two obtuse corners cannot share a join along the same side. Thus the outside population contains a tile with short height $-1$ or 1 serving an axis side and a tile with short height 1 or 3 serving the height-2 side.

Ordinary outside nodes, meaning all nodes except the height-zero core, each cost at least 74 tiles when occupied. The outside consists of whole short nodes. It can therefore have only one ordinary occupied node; the preceding requirement forces it to have height 1. Its population is exactly 74, since it is a multiple of 37. At most 27 tiles can have short node equal to the height-zero core. The $c^2$ side is covered entirely by long sides of height-1 tiles, using 37 of them. Of the length $1551+511=2062$ of the other two sides, long sides can cover at most $37^2=1369$. The short edges of the core tiles must cover at least 693.

Take the alternating signed length $(-1)^jL$ for orientation $\rho^jz^h$. On the two axial sides the positive boundary has headings $1,-\rho$; both have positive sign. On a CCW tile the signs of its $a$ and $b$ edges are opposite. Let $G$ be the number of positive $a$ signs minus the number of negative ones in the core. Nodewise cancellation at height 0 gives $(33-7)G\equiv2062\pmod{37}$, hence $G\equiv11\pmod{37}$. A core tile of positive sign can contribute only its $a$-edge to the two axial sides, and one of negative sign only its $b$-edge. Among at most 27 tiles with this congruence the maximum contribution is $19\cdot33+8\cdot7=683$ (the other possible value of $G$ is $-26$). This contradicts 693. The uniform exclusion follows. Since all twenty high positions have $h\ge41$, each is forbidden. Independently, all $h\ge91$ already violate the original number of occupied heights on this board.

**The eight lower replacements.** At a low anchor $h=0$ or 1, its branch at level $-4$ is also exterior-free. Different residues of $37^5x\bmod\pi$ give disjoint balls at that level, while integral translations do not change the ball. The four first-copy low anchors have four distinct residues. The corresponding eight replacements thus occupy four remote branches in pairs. None of the 3399 exterior tiles is in these branches; their coordinates have denominator at most 37 and valuation at least $-1$. Of the new positions only two low replacements per branch are possible in an actual tiling, by the high-position exclusion above.

Keeping even one low tile requires at least four occupied ancestor nodes among $-4,\ldots,0$. Its branch therefore needs at least 296 tiles, of which at most two can come from the 3427 packing. A full tiling retaining it must use at least 294 new positions and omit at least $294-(3431-3427)=290$ displayed positions. If all eight low replacements are kept, the three height-zero branches supply five ancestors each and the height-one branch supplies six. Among the 21 nodes in these four disjoint branches at most one can be empty. At least $20\cdot74=1480$ tiles are required, and at most eight can come from the displayed packing. This forces at least 1468 omissions. In particular **every full tiling must change at least 30** of the 3427 positions. If no replacement is retained, then in addition to these 28 omissions, at least one tile must be removed from each of the two disjoint [forbidden triples](../packing/TRIPLES.md). Both triples lie among the 3399 exterior placements retained when the two pentagons were repacked: triple A,B,C in the first copy and its integral translate in the second are outside the pentagons. The [coordinate test](../packing/check_triples.py) supplies their six explicit triangles. If any replacement is retained, the larger bound of 290 already applies.

Run the [small exact checker](verify.py) with assertions enabled. It reads the same 14 anchors as the full-board packing replay. These conclusions exclude placements, not all possible tilings of the board.
