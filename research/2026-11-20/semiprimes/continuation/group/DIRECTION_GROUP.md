# The F2 boundary word in the group of tile-edge directions

Let $a,b,c$ be positive integers with $\gcd(a,b)=1$ and $c^2=a^2+ab+b^2$. Put $\rho=e^{i\pi/3}$, $z=(a+b\rho)/c$, $H=b(2a+b)$ and $V=a(a+2b)$. In the axial plane the F2 board has vertices $0,H,V\rho$. Its third side has displacement $V\rho-H=c^2\rho z^2$ in the direction from $H$ to $V\rho$.

Divide each tile side into unit intervals. On any maximal straight run in a tiling, both banks partition the run into whole tile sides with integer lengths. All their endpoints are therefore at integer offsets from one end. Unit subdivisions agree across partial edge contacts.

Let $G$ have a generator $x_{h,j}$ for a directed unit step $z^h\rho^j$, for $h\in\mathbb Z$ and $j$ modulo six. Impose $x_{h,j+3}=x_{h,j}^{-1}$ and all the boundary relators of the two tile hands at every rotation and height. Write $A=x_{h,j}^a$. The triangle with vertices $0,az^h\rho^j,bz^h\rho^{j-2}$ has long-edge displacement $-cz^{h+1}\rho^j$ from the second to the third vertex. The triangle using $j+2$ instead has long-edge displacement $-cz^{h-1}\rho^j$. Their relators give
$$
x_{h+1,j}^c=x_{h,j-2}^{-b}x_{h,j}^a,\qquad
x_{h-1,j}^c=x_{h,j+2}^{-b}x_{h,j}^a. \tag{1}
$$
The corresponding half-turned relators give the same products in the reverse order. Consequently $x_{h,j}^a$ commutes with $x_{h,j\pm2}^b$. The same holds with $a,b$ exchanged by interchanging the indices. Write $p_j=x_{h,j}^{ab}$ at a fixed height $h$. Then $p_j$ commutes with both the $a$th and the $b$th powers of every $x_{h,k}$. Bézout's identity shows that $p_j$ commutes with every $x_{h,k}$.

Use the downward relation in (1) from height $h+1$ to height $h$ and then the upward relation from height $h$ to $h+1$. The commuting pairs on each right-hand side permit the indicated powers to be expanded, giving
$$
\begin{aligned}
 x_{h,j}^{c^2}
   &=(x_{h+1,j+2}^c)^{-b}(x_{h+1,j}^c)^a\\
   &=x_{h,j}^{b^2}x_{h,j+2}^{-ab}
     x_{h,j-2}^{-ab}x_{h,j}^{a^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $c^2-a^2-b^2=ab$ and the $ab$th powers commute at this height, it follows that
$$
x_{h,j}^{ab}x_{h,j-2}^{ab}x_{h,j+2}^{ab}=1. \tag{2}
$$

The counterclockwise board word is $W=x_{0,0}^H x_{2,1}^{c^2}x_{0,1}^{-V}$. Apply the upward relation twice:
$$
\begin{aligned}
x_{2,1}^{c^2}
   &=(x_{1,-1}^c)^{-b}(x_{1,1}^c)^a\\
   &=x_{0,0}^{-b^2}x_{0,-1}^{-2ab}x_{0,1}^{a^2}.
\end{aligned}
$$
Using $H-b^2=V-a^2=2ab$ and $x_{0,-1}^{-1}=x_{0,2}$, $x_{0,1}^{-1}=x_{0,4}$ gives
$$
W=(x_{0,0}^{ab}x_{0,2}^{ab}x_{0,4}^{ab})^2=1
$$
by (2). In particular every direction-only group labelling satisfying all the unit-tile relators evaluates the F2 boundary to the identity, including on the 3431 and 5063 boards.

## Labels that also depend on position

Take the presentation complex of $G$, with one vertex, one edge per oriented generator up to reversal, and a face for each tile relator. Sending $x_{h,j}$ to its geometric displacement defines a homomorphism to the additive group generated by all the steps. Every tile relator has zero displacement. In the associated covering complex the vertices are the reachable positions, an edge records its actual start and end, and there is a face for every translate of each tile relator. A nullhomotopy of $W$ in the presentation complex lifts from the board origin. Its boundary is the positioned board boundary.

Thus any group or groupoid labelling on the positioned edges with inverse labels on reversed edges and trivial products around **every** translated tile also gives a trivial product around the board. The same conclusion holds when labels use only a residue class of the position, by pulling them back to the position cover.

The lifted diagram allows translated faces anywhere in the plane and allows either sign of a relator in a van Kampen diagram. The identity leaves open labellings imposed only on placements inside the board. A tiling also requires positive tile areas, containment and disjoint interiors. The signed diagram by itself supplies no such geometric filling.
