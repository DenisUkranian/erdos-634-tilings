# An oversized staircase band cannot be repaired inside its auxiliary triangle at v=1

Use the primitive $v=1$ tile $a=(u^2-1)/g$, $b=(2u+1)/g$, $c=(u^2+u+1)/g$, $g=\gcd(3,u-1)$, for $u\ge4$ with $a>b$. Let $K=\lfloor a/u\rfloor$. Consider a step $ar-bs=ct$ of the [unit staircase](https://github.com/DenisUkranian/erdos-634-tilings/blob/ea420d780718a9e4a1801dfcb66c8f8975e48863/research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/staircase/STAIRCASE.md) having $b<r\le c-b$ and $1\le s\le c-a$. Put $k=r-b$. In the signed construction the horizontal band overshoots its grid by a $k$-fold similar triangle. Subtracting this triangle from the positive auxiliary $t$-grid gives the notch with radial intercepts $(ta,tc)$ outside and $(kc,ka)$ inside. These integral loads give a genuine nondegenerate quadrilateral.

**Theorem, using the exterior stopped-run input.** This individual notch quadrilateral cannot be tiled by the given tile, allowing reflections, other directions and T-junctions. The argument holds for each oversized step separately. It applies independently of the order of steps.

On the ray of the original long side, its remaining true boundary segment has length
$$
tc-ka=ar-bs-(r-b)a=b(a-s).
$$
Its endpoint at the original $t$-triangle vertex has angle $\alpha$ opposite the side $a$. At the other endpoint the removed triangle has angle $120^\circ$, leaving $60^\circ=\alpha+\beta$. In particular neither sector contains a $120^\circ$ tile angle. At the $60^\circ$ endpoint an $\alpha$ tile on a long edge has its neighboring $\beta$ protecting its $b$-ray. At the single-$\alpha$ endpoint the genuine adjacent exterior side has length $tb>Kb$. To see the last inequality, $t\ge[a(b+1)-b(c-a)]/c>a/u$. After multiplying the numerator of the strict comparison by $g^2$ one obtains $u^4+(g-2)u^3-7u^2-(g+2)u+1$, bounded below by $u^4-u^3-7u^2-5u+1>0$ for $u\ge4$, since $u^2-u-7\ge5$.

By the ordinary exterior stopped-run argument, the displayed segment needs at least $K+1$ long edges. This invokes [the stated $v=1$ geometric input](https://github.com/DenisUkranian/erdos-634-tilings/blob/ea420d780718a9e4a1801dfcb66c8f8975e48863/research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/counting/inputs/F2_V1_PURITY.md) and its cited local lemma. In contrast, write $b(a-s)=Xa+Yb+Zc$ from any boundary inventory. Since $s>0$, the length is less than $ab$, and therefore $X,Z<b$. Here $a+c=ub$ and $a$ is coprime to $b$, so $X\equiv Z\pmod b$, hence $X=Z$. This forces $Y+uZ=a-s$, and $Z\le\lfloor(a-s)/u\rfloor\le K$. This excludes the notch. Both radial remainders are positive: $s\le c-a<b<a$, and the bounds $r\le c-b$, $s\le c-a$ give $ta-kc=b[c^2-r(a+b)-as]/c\ge b(2c-2a-b)>0$, since $4c^2-(2a+b)^2=3b^2$.

For the (33,7,37) tile, the split $(4,3,3)+(26,1,23)$ gives $(t,k)=(23,19)$ and a 168-tile notch with protected side $224=32\cdot7$. For (35,13,43), the split $(8,5,5)+(22,3,17)$ gives $(17,9)$ and a 208-tile notch with protected side $416=32\cdot13$. The same argument also covers the known equal-step notches of areas 105 and 117, and the one-step notches of 147 and 195. The project already has independent [complete geometric exclusion certificates](https://github.com/DenisUkranian/erdos-634-tilings/blob/ea420d780718a9e4a1801dfcb66c8f8975e48863/research/2026-11-20/semiprimes/continuation/notches/NOTCH_ENUMERATION.md) for these last four counts. The new conclusion relies on the indicated manuscript lemma.

A surgery that opens an adjacent region can destroy the protected side or alter its endpoint sectors. This proof does not cover that operation or decide an original F2 triangle.

Combining this with [the relaxed step obstruction](REVERSED_STEPS.md) eliminates the *entire* individual-notch repair procedure for residual odd $v=1$ parameters. Any relaxed list has some $r>b$. Totals force $r\le c-b<c$ and $s\le c-a$. Also $s$ cannot vanish in that load because $c\mid ar$ and $\gcd(a,c)=1$. Thus that one auxiliary triangle already has the forbidden notch. The conclusion permits either sign on the other auxiliary steps and arbitrarily many loads. It relies on the exterior stopped-run input as above.
