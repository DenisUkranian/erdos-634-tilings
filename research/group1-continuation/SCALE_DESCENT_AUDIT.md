# Why scale-one forcing does not yet classify the W/beta spectrum

7 October 2026. Internal mathematical audit and continuation.
`full_Erdos634_solved: false`.

The established construction gives every scale in
`v + <u,v>` for each primitive W and beta tile. This note does not claim
that the set is necessary. It isolates a geometric obstruction to
extending the scale-one exclusion by the same induction.

## 1. The first c/a exchange is an actual tiling

Let `0<u<v`, `gcd(u,v)=1`, and

    a=uv, b=v²-u², c=v².

The angle between sides a and c of the tile is beta. Thus, for unit
vectors e,f with angle beta, the parallelogram generated by ae and cf
splits along its difference diagonal into two copies of the tile.
The same holds for the parallelogram generated by ce and af.

**Exchange lemma.** There is an actual tiled parallelogram containing
a seam whose two complete banks are exactly u c-edges and v a-edges.
Its tile count is `2(u+v)`.

**Proof.** Put `H=uc=va`. Take the parallelogram generated by
`(a+c)e` and `Hf`, and cut it along the segment from ae to `ae+Hf`.
The left strip has width a and consists of u cells generated by ae,cf.
The right strip has width c and consists of v cells generated by ce,af.
Split each cell along the difference diagonal of length b. The cells
have disjoint interiors and exhaust the parallelogram, proving the
claim. Since `gcd(u,v)=1`, an equality `ic=ja`, with `0<=i<=u` and
`0<=j<=v`, occurs only at the two seam endpoints. In particular the
unequal bank partitions cause legitimate T-junctions, with no overlap.
For u=1 the c-bank simply has no internal endpoint. QED.

The short-chain arithmetic in the scale-one proof says that

    nc = Aa + Bb + Cc, 0<n<u

forces `A=B=0`. At the very next integer n=u the exchange lemma
realizes `A=v, B=C=0` geometrically. Therefore that threshold is sharp
even among actual local tilings; it cannot be enlarged using only
seam integrality, positive lengths, local flatness, or T-junction rules.

For (u,v)=(2,3), the example has tile sides (6,5,9), a ten-tile
parallelogram of side lengths 15 and 18 at angle beta, and seam banks
`9+9 = 6+6+6`.

## 2. Exact location of the missing global argument

The scale-one W proof in `../../docs/prime-case-candidate.md` starts
with the exact boundary count `(A,B,C)=(0,u,u)` on the side OC of
length u(2v²-u²). Its first column and subsequent column-completion
arguments force the completed column height n to satisfy `n<u`.
Short c-chain purity then forbids an a-edge on the opposite bank.

At larger scale the OC boundary can contain a-edges, so its first
tile need not even have the same orientation. Even where a normal
c-column is forced, its height can reach u. The exchange lemma then
shows exactly why the no-opposite-a conclusion is unavailable.
Similarly, the scale-one beta proof uses boundary and column length
bounds that are not preserved by enlarging the target.

An exact-spectrum proof by descent would need a new global theorem:
from every tiled W or beta target outside the base cases, extract or
replace a whole tiled collar so that a smaller tiled target at scale
m-u or m-v remains, with the appropriate admissibility conditions.
The ability to add a collar does not prove that an arbitrary tiling
contains such a removable collar. The local exchange above neither
proves nor disproves this global descent statement.

## 3. The first (6,5,9) gap passes present boundary checks

For W with (u,v)=(2,3), the known positive scale set is
`3+<2,3> = {3,5,6,7,...}`. Scale one is excluded by the internally
audited scale-one theorem. Scales two and four are not decided here.

At scale two, N=56 and the side lengths are 54,56,30. The complete
nonnegative side-count solutions `(A,B,C)` with at least two c-edges
are:

| Side length | Counts of (a,b,c) edges |
|---|---|
| 30 | (2,0,2) |
| 56 | (4,1,3), (1,1,5), (3,4,2), (0,4,4) |
| 54 | (6,0,2), (3,0,4), (0,0,6), (2,3,3), (1,6,2) |

These follow directly from `6A+5B+9C=L`, with `A,B>=0,C>=2`.
Thus the universal two-c-edge theorem and integer boundary lengths
do not exclude this instance. These counts are necessary tests, not
a geometric certificate of existence.

The unchanged exact convex-corner search
`research/uniform-reduction/exact_search.py 56 --branch G1-W
--seconds 240 --max-nodes 1000000` returned **INCOMPLETE** after
10,595 nodes, with at most 44 of 56 tiles placed. It found neither a
tiling nor an exhaustive exclusion. A separate 120-second fixed-tile
W test at (u,v,m)=(1,3,2), N=68, was also **INCOMPLETE** after 3,291
nodes, maximum depth 53/68. The integer 68 already has a classical
tiling; that run concerned only the specified W shape and tile.
No conclusion about a missing scale is inferred from either timeout.

The present result is the sharp, geometrically realized local exchange
lemma and the identified need for a global descent argument. Equality
of the fixed-tile spectra with `v+<u,v>` remains unproved.
