## External statement checked, not silently strengthened

Beeson and Zhang, Rationality of certain triangle tilings, arXiv:2604.01314v1,
Theorem 1.2, states rational side ratios for a tiling tile that is nonsimilar
to the target, nonright, and has incommensurable angles. These exclusions
are essential. This permits primitive integer normalization ONLY after the
excluded cases have been handled separately. It is not a theorem of
geometric sufficiency, and does not certify an implemented thirteen-row
arithmetic enumerator. The abstract alone would be inadequate for that use.

The HTML statement and the listed exceptions in Section 6 were inspected.
The public PDF rendering tool failed to fetch screenshots; no claim of a
new visual audit of its tables or diagrams is made. This phase does not
independently reprove that external theorem or all of its cited prerequisites.
