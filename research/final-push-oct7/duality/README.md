# Positive filling: a local obstruction and a noncommutative audit

7 October 2026. Research directed by Denis Paliy, with ChatGPT assistance.

**Neither the full classification nor 154 or 4830 is decided here.**

The [nine-triangle construction](LOCAL_MULTIPLICITY.md) proves that even an
exact, positive, twofold covering by congruent 120-degree triangles need
not split into two onefold coverings **locally near one vertex**. Thus
local sheet selection cannot be assumed in a proposed conversion from
positive rational fillings to tilings. The construction is not asserted
to extend to a twofold covering of a complete triangular target.

The [group calculation](NONCOMMUTATIVE_BOUNDARY.md) examines an invariant
that retains the order of the unit boundary atoms. Every F3 boundary word
is trivial in every quotient of prime exponent. Quotients whose finite
exponent is coprime to abc have abelian image and supply no additional
obstruction. This does not prove triviality in the full tile group, nor
does triviality imply a positive tiling.

`check_duality.py` checks the local angular coverage and induced odd cycle
at exact rational angle parameters. It also checks an explicit
nonabelian Heisenberg quotient for the tile (24,11,31). The written
arguments establish the general statements; the finite checks are not
substitutes for those proofs.
