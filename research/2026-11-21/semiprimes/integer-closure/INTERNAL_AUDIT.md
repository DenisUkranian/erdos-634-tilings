# Independent internal review of the integer and physical continuation

21 November 2026. **Both original target counts remain open.**

## Mandatory long banks

The root researcher derived and implemented exact actual-line prefix
inversion, whole-neighbor choices, and the necessary 2-SAT system. Two
independent parallel reviews checked the recurrence, rational normalization,
two-apex construction, nonoverlap clauses, and completeness scope.

The geometric reviewer independently established the neutral common-long-side
pair decomposition and the fixed-sign formula for both neighboring choices.
This led to the stronger paired positive/negative population reserve and
the modular charge transport bound across even heights. The root reviewed
their formulas, the use of only proved required nodes, and the maximum of
the two tree budgets. Adding those budgets would incorrectly count pairs
twice and is not used.

The implementation reviewer independently reconstructed a complete 143
tiling and tested exact rotated and fractionally translated copies. The
reconstructed net even long edges agree with those in the complete tiling;
the remaining 36 even tiles form 18 neutral pairs. A separate complete 143
certificate has 91 odd tiles, two mandatory even tiles, and 25 pairs. Both
full tilings remain positive controls.

The 2-SAT implementation is compared with exhaustive truth assignments on
28,933 small formulas and checked on 2000-variable chains. The geometric
cases distinguish touching from strict interior intersection. These checks
validate the implementation and stated controls; the universal argument
remains the written mathematical proof.

## Signed integer image

The arithmetic researcher constructed complete endpoint kernels and an
independent standard-library determinant verifier. The root reviewed the
feature-coordinate chart, the necessary charge potential, and the lattice
index argument. In particular, the proof does not need the exhibited kernels
to generate the entire endpoint kernel: their feature span already has
index `c`, which is the largest allowed lattice.

As an independent additional check, the root computed the Hermite normal
form of the **entire** supplied feature-column matrix with SymPy. The
resulting square bases had dimensions 75 and 87 and determinants 37 and
43, respectively. This auxiliary check agrees with the seven exact-minor
certificates and is not needed by the standard-library proof replay.

The signed native witnesses contain negative multiplicities. Their exact
endpoint/gate verification does not provide a physical positive certificate.
The theorem concerns integer linear congruences of the specified periodic
projection; positivity and actual geometry remain additional constraints.

## Countercontrols and avoided overclaims

The charged-cell researcher found and exactly verified the rigid 13-tile
unit-charge cylinder control and its compact variants. The root independently
checked their whole-neighbor obstructions. The separated lift fails
same-side long-edge capacity. The compact lift passes that capacity but
forces a long edge for which both possible whole neighbors overlap an
already placed odd tile.

The same necessary test rejects the specific old balanced hexagon and
contained 16-tile control. A different six-tile two-fan patch passes, with
zero unreduced short current. This positive control prevents an incorrect
inference that every balanced six-tile cell is excluded. The remaining
5063 parameter list is unchanged.

The compact-lift scan and the target mixed-integer diagnostic time limits
are exploratory provenance only. They are not used to assert absence of
all lifts, all unit-charge cells, or either original tiling. The unit-charge
bound `n>=3c` at the two target shapes remains unresolved.

## Essential completeness boundary

Reconstruction for one **complete** odd Euclidean cell cannot be changed by
another cell in the same direction height, because their actual `c`-step
orbits are disjoint. It may therefore give a valid cell obstruction.

The neutral-pair decomposition and global paired reserve require the
**complete all-odd family**. They must not be applied to an unfinished
partial odd placement or to just one of several odd cells. The test passes
some whole configurations and rejects others; no exhaustive enumeration
of every admissible target odd packing is claimed.

This is internal independent review, not external peer review or a Lean
formalization. Exact replay records identify the checked finite content.
