# An exterior-free mixed gate cannot have 22 owners at 3431

Let a complete tiling of the target F2 board have an exterior-free gate with both signed far populations E,O nonzero in the standard frame. At 3431 this gate requires **at least 24 actual unmatched owners**, without any upper bound on the total unmatched population. Consequently the global [five-region bound](FOUR_GATES.md) improves to **M−Z ≥ 173** in the mixed case. The unit-alternating and 5063 bounds have their own hypotheses.

## An isolated polygon forced by the two charges

Write E=37e,O=37o. The ordinary and alternating bank characters bound the cost below by the maximum of 11|e+o| and 63|e−o|. Thus below 24 only e=o=±1 is possible, the cost is even, and only 22 is left. The [Jordan circuit decomposition](../../../2026-11-26/semiprimes/charged-contour/README.md) applies to this physical cut. Every circuit has zero alternating charge because its length is less than 63. Each circuit's two signed parities are equal multiples of 37, including the orientation sign. At least one has nonzero charge, which costs at least 22 by its ordinary character. It uses every edge. Its positive enclosed area is the whole far population.

In the oriented chart let the banks have vectors Dρʲ and 37ρʲ for D=33+7 conjugate(ρ). All ordinary character contributions agree. A half-turn makes all headings even. Each bank has eleven owners by its signed character. Closure gives precisely three inventories, related by rotations of 120 degrees. Index low letters 0–5 and high letters 6–11 by the heading modulo six. Choose counts of 7 low-2, 4 high-4, 4 low-4 and 7 high-0. The replay enumerates the low heading triple, then solves the two closure equations and the high count for the other triple.

In the order-33 projection low-2 and high-4 lie in one factor with weights +7,−37. Low-4 and high-0 lie in a different factor with opposite signs and the same two magnitudes. In either factor the only zero subinventories are empty or full: check 7i−37j modulo 33 on the rectangles (7,4) and (4,7). The first monochromatic run to disappear in a cyclic reduction therefore contains its entire factor. Rotating the word puts all letters of the first factor before all of the other. In order 7 these two segments use respectively the factor sets {2,1} and {1,0}. Their products must both belong to the intersection, factor 1. The seven low-2 occurrences must be contiguous in the first segment since the total of four high-4 occurrences is less than seven and none of their nonempty runs cancels. The seven high-0 occurrences must likewise be contiguous in the second segment. Denoting low and high by L,H gives the word
$$H_4^i L_2^7 H_4^{4-i} L_4^j H_0^7 L_4^{4-j},\qquad 0\le i,j\le4.$$
Its exact shoelace area is 74(j−i) tile units, in particular at most 296.

## The terminal population makes the list finite

The signed short population of either parity sums to ±37. That parity has an odd number of blocks of 37 tiles and needs at least three, by the [ordinary-lift population bound](../../../2026-11-26/semiprimes/joint-cell-tree/TREE.md). Each nonempty exterior-free subtree has an occupied root, and each occupied ordinary lift costs at least two blocks.

Choose an occupied leaf in the far subtree. If it has nonzero charge, the terminal inequality n ≥ (91/22)|G| forces it to have at least five blocks. With the opposite parity needing three, this uses all eight available blocks. There are then just two adjacent occupied lifts with populations 111 and 185. If the leaf is balanced, set aside its at least two blocks: both signed sums still require three each. Equality forces a balanced 74-tile leaf and exactly one 111-tile lift of each parity. Neither charged lift can be terminal. The three occupied lifts thus form a chain from the far root with populations 111,111,74 in that order. This accounts for the possibility of a genuine balanced descendant.

In both cases n=296, which requires i=0,j=4. The entire physical boundary is forced up to bank choice and isometry. Remove the eleven known owners on the far bank. In far-short coordinates, a directed low long edge on that bank is replaced by its two short steps aUⱼ and bUⱼ₋₁ in that order, whereas a near long side is already a c-step in the far short chart. This follows by placing the third vertex on the left of the oriented far edge. Exchanging the banks is a reflection across their axis bisector with reversed traversal. In the new low/far chart this maps headings to 3−j and interchanges low and high. The [existing contour repair](../remote-sections/contours.py) checks the owners for interior overlap, cancels opposite unit steps, and obtains a simple 21-corner polygon of area 285 tiles. The two repaired polygons are congruent by a symmetry of the triangular lattice and an integral translation, verified on their complete cyclic vertex lists. Every remaining boundary side has the far root marker, including at physical T-junctions. The remaining root budget is 100 tiles.

## Reproducing the exclusion

Use the section tests of the [three-lift theorem](../remote-sections/THREE_LIFTS.md). At two lifts a neighbor of the root has two possible hands and 37 possible translates per hand. Objects in the repaired polygon are either actual half-turn pairs in a short mesh or singles with their long endpoints in the adjacent short mesh. The unmarked rational section test already rejects all 74 choices. For three lifts enumerate a chain with those same 74 first-step and 74 second-step possibilities in the respective local coordinates. Returning to the identical parent marker reverses the edge and cannot create a new lift. In the preliminary relaxation retain both directions of singles crossing the root gate and all root pairs, and permit arbitrary intervening nonroot material on each section. The exact second test uses three distinct labels on all remaining candidate objects. Consecutive intervals in a tiled section must have the same marker at their common physical endpoint. Section failure rules out that case with arbitrary T-junctions. A case that only reaches a pruning limit must be retained.

The [exact replay](short_mixed.py) enumerates the inventories and candidate words, rebuilds the contour, tests both numbers of lifts, and applies the same section engine to both complete 143 tilings. From this directory run `python3 short_mixed.py --workers 6 | diff - SHORT_MIXED.json`. The [record](SHORT_MIXED.json) includes the unexcluded case list. No construction or unrestricted impossibility result for either original board follows from this contact obstruction.

## The corresponding minimum ordinary gate at 5063

At 5063 the ordinary floor for e=o=±1 is 42. If it is attained, exactly one positive Jordan circuit carries the charge by the same decomposition argument. Each bank contributes 21 even headings after normalization. Enumerate the three low counts totaling 21 and match their physical displacement against that of a high triple totaling 21. Seven inventories remain. Applying the order-35 factor recurrence with all high headings reversed relative to the alternating alphabet gives an upper bound of 349160 on doubled axial area in every case. The population is an even multiple of 43. It is therefore at most 688. For the lower bound, a charged terminal needs at least seven blocks by the 83/13 terminal inequality, and the opposite parity needs at least three. A balanced terminal needs at least two in addition to the three needed on each parity. Consequently the only possible populations are **344, 430, 516, 602 and 688**. At 344 the only topology is a chain with short populations 129,129,86, the last balanced. In particular if no neutral gate is occupied, the minimum is 430. These are necessary restrictions; the 42-owner gate remains open.
