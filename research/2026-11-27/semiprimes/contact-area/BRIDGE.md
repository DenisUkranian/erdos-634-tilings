# A mixed 73-owner gate carrying half of the 5063 board

In the standard frame let an exterior-free gate with nonzero E and O satisfy |E−O|=43. If it has 73 unmatched owners and its far subtree contains at least half the board, its directed boundary, up to the isometries and choice of first letter below, is one of **four explicit words**. Their areas in tile units are 2623, 2537, 2537 and 2537. Two different physical banks, all translations and arbitrary deeper trees are allowed. This is a necessary local classification, not a list of tilings.

In particular, for any mixed unit-charge gate containing half the 5063 board the [five-region inequality](FOUR_GATES.md) strengthens to **M−Z ≥ 231**. The improvement uses the physical position of the axial node at equality. Z counts the actual owners on neutral exterior-free gates with E=O=0.

## From the two boundary groups to four words

The [physical circuit theorem](../../../2026-11-26/semiprimes/charged-contour/README.md) gives one positive Jordan polygon. The [two character bounds](ONE_DEFECT.md) force bank magnitudes 64 and 1 and bank sizes 64 and 9. Use the half-turn and the [bank-exchange symmetry](SATURATION.md) for oriented words to put the large bank on the low alphabet with L=64,H=−1. With the alternative difference sign use a half-turn first. Exchanging banks by a reflection and reversing the traversal takes (L,H) to (−H,−L) and preserves both group identities and the positive area. All physical orientations of both banks are allowed in the containment check below.

Write ρ=exp(iπ/3) and D=35+13 conjugate(ρ). The directed moves numbered 0 through 5 are
$$D,\quad43\rho,\quad D\rho^2,\quad43,\quad D\rho^4,\quad43\rho^5.$$
Move 3 is the reversed high step. For low counts on headings 0,2,4 totaling 64, closure fixes the high displacement. Its heading character −1 fixes the three signed counts on axes 0,1,2. All three solutions are even rotations of (17,23,24) low and (4,2,−3) high. All use nine net high owners, leaving no opposite pairs. The normalized inventory in letter order is (17,2,23,4,24,3).

In the order-35 projection name the factors A,B,C using A={0,5}, B={2,1}, C={4,3}. Choose a generator on each axis so the low weight is one. The high weights are +6,+6,−6 respectively because 43/13 ≡ 6 mod 35. Thus the A and B powers are all positive and each sums to exactly 35. The C powers sum to zero, with positive and negative totals 24 and −24. Project away C. To reduce to the identity the two-factor cyclic word must have a monochromatic zero run. Such a run uses every occurrence of its factor. Hence A precedes B after a cyclic rotation to the first A. Now delete B. All C strings between successive A letters must have sum zero: before an A run can vanish it must contain every A letter, so every intervening C string must vanish. The same argument deleting A applies between successive B letters. Each such C sum vanishes over the integers because its magnitude is at most 24. Starting at C level zero, all A steps lie at zero and all B steps lie at a common integer level u between −24 and 24.

[bridge.cpp](bridge.cpp) uses the exact [single-factor recurrence](SATURATION.md) at order 13, preserving the order A*B* in the projection and the starting C level of each subword. An append or concatenation is allowed only at matching levels; displacement fixes the level change and the shoelace cross term. A proper nonempty subword with zero spatial displacement is impossible for a Jordan polygon. The relaxation upper-bounds the maximum at every u. Since n is an odd multiple of 43, half the board requires n ≥ 2537. Only u=−24 and u=−18 meet the doubled-area threshold 455·2537.

The [second calculation](bridge.py) uses literal free-product stacks, independently of the factor recurrence. Split after the C bridge from A to B. At −24 that bridge consists of all four negative letters, and the return bridge of all 24 positive letters. At −18 the bridge contains either four negatives and six positives, or three negatives and no positives. In the latter case the remaining negative is either in the return bridge with 24 positives, or in one internal zero gap with six positives, leaving 18 positives for the return bridge. These are all possibilities. Enumerate each half by permuting its A or B letters and inserting each specified gap. Match mutually inverse order-13 stacks. Since the two displacements sum to zero their shoelace scores add. Of the 70 literal matches at the two levels, just four reach the threshold. The words and scores are in [BRIDGE.json](BRIDGE.json). Their presence is not sufficient for tileability.

## Why the mixed gate cannot be at the five-region endpoint

The exhaustive equality replay in [four_gates.py](four_gates.py) retains every profile below or at 229 in the mixed unit case. There is just one: (e,o)=(−1,−2), the mixed far root is odd, attachment is in the axial region, and the deepest nonzero short charge is −c at an even lift. Native signed pairs and owner counts are (0,d,a+b) and (0,c,c). All remaining regional and shell charges are zero, and every owner outside the four distinguished gates belongs to a neutral gate. These facts hold for M−Z=229 with Z unrestricted. In particular the mixed gate has 73 owners. Its near signed count is c−2d=−1. If its parent were off the native path, the only distinguished bank there could be this near bank, since the deepest gate is strictly within the mixed subtree. The neutral banks contribute zero. This violates divisibility by c of the signed short charge at a nonnative lift. Thus its parent is the axial lift C.

The first native gate has exactly the two required directions in the low-short bank. The physical [displacement census](../../../2026-11-26/semiprimes/boundary-word/remote.py) gives signed triples (0,0,0) and (0,−b,a). All steps are strictly monotone in x in the axial chart, so the chain is one path from Y to X without additional cycles. In middle coordinates, after rotation by the inverse of z=(a+bρ)/c, the steps have length c on directions 4 and 5 with counts a and b. The slant has net displacement opposite to the path. Its supported word projects to the single axis of direction 5 modulo a, and to that of direction 4 modulo b, since its displacement is the sum of the two path vectors with opposite sign. The slant itself is straight and its physical height is two, so its projected word at either order indeed lies in a single factor.

In order a, the a direction-4 steps must cancel together. No nonempty block of the at most b<a direction-5 steps can cancel there since c is invertible. Hence the path has form 5ⁱ 4ᵃ 5ᵇ⁻ⁱ. In order b, the middle block is nonzero since a and c are coprime to b. Consequently i is either 0 or b. Starting with direction 5 would immediately leave the board on the exterior side of the slant. The other path passes through K=(ab,ab) in the axial chart. The slant region is the triangle YKX with c² tiles by area; the axial region is the concave quadrilateral OXKY.

Write X=(1079,0), Y=(0,2135), K=(455,455). In the positive quadrant the axial region is described by
$$48x+13y\le13\cdot2135\quad\hbox{or}\quad35x+48y\le35\cdot1079.$$
It is downward closed in each coordinate. If an oriented set of vertices fits by translation, it still fits after both its coordinate minima are translated to zero. The mixed gate meets C, so one of its two alphabets has the axial directions, the other those of a neighboring height ±1. For the normalized four polygons it suffices to test multiplication by ρʲ times 1, z or its conjugate, with or without complex conjugation. These tests include both possibilities for which alphabet meets C, even after the bank exchange or choice of far hand. Evaluate the two displayed slacks on every vertex after translation and scaling by 43 to keep them integral. Take their minimum at each vertex, then their maximum over vertices, and finally the minimum across orientations. The four excesses are **283413, 283413, 283413, 276146**, all strictly positive. Every contour therefore has a vertex outside the physical axial region. Finally M and N have the same parity by half-turn pairing, and Z is even by the neutral bank characters. This gives the claimed 231.

For 3431 the equality profile at M−Z=203 has the same native cut with its own parameters. The axial quadrilateral has 2062 tiles. At least 40 native owners and 15 near mixed owners lie there but outside the mixed subtree, giving n ≤ 2007 and hence n ≤ 1961 by odd-multiple rounding. In particular a mixed unit gate enclosing more than 1961 on that board needs M−Z ≥ 205. These conclusions require none of the native two-height population bounds.
