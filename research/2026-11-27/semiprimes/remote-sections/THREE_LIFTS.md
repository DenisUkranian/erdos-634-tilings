# A short two-owner interface cannot hide a third occupied lift at 3431

Consider an occupied exterior-free branch with a two-owner bank in an arbitrary complete 3431 tiling. If it contains an occupied descendant, then **its entering edge needs at least 32 unmatched tiles**. The [four-block bound](README.md) raises this to 36 when the branch has 148 tiles. The corresponding general bound for 5063 is 38.

The [two-owner word census](../../../2026-11-26/semiprimes/terminal-contact/TWO_OWNER.md) gives all possibilities with fewer than 32 owners. At population 74 the root is terminal. The 148-tile nonterminal possibilities were excluded in the [four-block proof](README.md). Only population 222 with 24 owners remains. Its two outer circuits leave 22 or 2 far owners. After their removal each connected boundary has the root as physical side marker throughout. High sides are near-owner long sides with root marker. The shorts exposed by removing the low owners have that same marker. Cancelling unit edges preserves it on every remaining boundary segment.

If the branch has two occupied lifts, [the two-mesh section test](slices.cpp) rejects all 74 neighboring lattices for each outer contour. Each would have to supply at least 74 tiles. With three occupied lifts, all three have exactly 74 tiles, by the ordinary population floor. Four occupied lifts exceed 222. Since every path to an occupied descendant has occupied ancestors, the three form either a path from the root or a root with two children. In particular the residual root has just 52 or 72 tiles, according to the outer word.

## Marking the ends of an interval

Slice along a line avoiding all tile vertices. At each junction of two successive intervals the two objects share positive physical contact. Their incident sides therefore have the same lifted marker, also in a tiling with T-junctions. A pair presents its short lift on every side. A triangular single presents its short lift on its two shorts and the adjacent lift on its long side. Each interval in a necessary partition now goes from a lower ordinate and marker to an upper ordinate and marker. Both extreme markers on each band of the residual are the root. Every transition in the interval graph must preserve the marker at its common endpoint. For the definitive three-mesh test use units scaled by $c^2$. All three short lattices have integral vertices in this frame. Half-integral probes avoid them.

At the root the $2c$ possible neighbors have a hand and a residue. For two children take all unordered distinct pairs of these neighbors. For a path choose one such neighbor and then a neighbor in its own coordinates. With $u_h=a+b\rho^h$, $h=\pm1$, the first child's lattice has origin $c^2t$ and basis multiplier $cu_h$ in the scaled frame. A grandchild has origin $c^2t+kcu_h$ and basis multiplier $u_hu_s$. This follows by applying the same coset parametrization in the child's short chart. When the last step returns to the projected parent cell it is exactly the reverse edge in the covering tree and cannot give a third lift. Remaining configurations are tested even where other physical restrictions could remove them.

## A relaxation that charges forced root tiles

Most topologies can be ruled out before inspecting the second generation. Keep the root pairs, the root singles going to the proposed immediate children, and the child singles whose long marker is the root. Regard all nonroot markers as a single label. Permit motion from any nonroot endpoint to any later nonroot endpoint anywhere in the same section band. This can insert arbitrary unknown material, ignore its cost, and merge distinct branches. Every actual section still has an allowed path. Indeed each interval presenting the root marker at an endpoint comes from one of the retained kinds of object. Any interval omitted from the list has nonroot markers at both ends. Even though the relaxation uses just a scale-$c$ catalog, no actual three-lift vertex lies on a half-integral probe at that scale: its denominator divides the odd number $c$.

Sweep the intervals by increasing lower ordinate. On the root label retain the reachable ordinates. On the nonroot label retain its first reachable ordinate $u$; all greater ordinates are then reachable. Sweep backwards by decreasing upper ordinate to get the last co-reachable nonroot ordinate $v$. Each physical interval on a possible complete path must be forward reachable at its lower end and backward reachable at its upper end. The free motion can occupy only the span from $u$ to $v$. On every open gap between interval endpoints, count the retained crossing objects and one extra possibility wherever this span is active. If a physical object is the only possibility there, it is compulsory. Count each such root triangle once and each compulsory root pair twice, across all lines and passes. Discard objects with positive-area overlap with a compulsory object; overlapping compulsory objects contradict a tiling. Convex separation uses exact signed determinants. The 52- or 72-tile residual root budget can therefore fail with the geometry of deeper branches completely relaxed.

For each topology still allowed, enumerate actual pairs in its three lattices and singles on its two edges. Use three separate marker labels with no free motion. Delete every crossing object excluded by a section. If the combined capacities of surviving candidates at either nonroot vertex fall below 74, that topology is impossible. A pair contributes two to this upper bound even when it overlaps other candidates counted there.

[THREE_LIFTS.json](THREE_LIFTS.json) records both words and the counts at each stage. In the topology column 0 denotes siblings and 1 denotes a chain. Each chain entry in the relaxed pass represents all second-step choices. Outcome 0 retains a case for the next pass, 1 indicates a disconnected section, 2 a root budget violation, 3 incompatible forced objects, 4 insufficient nonroot capacity, and 5 a reversed edge. All counts refer to deterministic finite tests with integer arithmetic.

Run `python three_lifts.py --workers 4 > local-three.json` and compare it with the recorded file. The worker count changes only elapsed time. The [control](controls_marked.py) reconstructs side markers on both complete 143 tilings. The exact test retains all 83 actual objects through 198 and 181 sections. In a second test it removes every interior object wholly beyond the axial lift and applies the relaxed rule to the remaining 34 and 41 objects. It retains every actual boundary object on both boards.

Consequently, in the global signed and area reserve in [README.md](README.md), each nonterminal neutral two-owner branch at 3431 can be priced at 32 on its own edge. The earlier price 36 still applies at 148 tiles. This is independent of its parent's or child's bank size on any other edge.
