  criterion holds for every primitive ordered tile with2<a/b<2725/1139.

  at every positive integer multiplier. A complete finite certificate and
  exact Frobenius estimate prove the uniform statement. The endpoint fails
  this recipe, without implying geometric nonexistence there.

  tile, a three-placement submatrix of the actual atomic-current matrix

  argument. Special triangular-boundary feasibility remains a separate issue.
