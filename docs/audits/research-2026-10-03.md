## Results selected for publication

The [separate internal review](october-theorems.md) reconstructs the universal arguments, identifies the external classification inputs, and records fresh executions.

For `N≡6 (mod16)`, `3∤N`, and no prime `p≡7 (mod8)` occurring to an odd exponent, the [uniform-sectors theorem](../../research/uniform-sectors/PROOF.md) proves

\[
N\text{ is admissible}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
N=(2v^2-u^2)(3v^2-u^2)t^2,
\quad 0<u<v,\ \gcd(u,v)=1,\ t\ge1.
\]

Necessity comes from an exhaustive residue collapse followed by the alpha-branch norm obstruction; sufficiency is the existing geometric QP construction. No W/beta scale-one exclusion is used. The prime-exponent restriction must not be dropped.

The [square-class saturation theorem](../../research/square-class-saturation/PROOF.md) chooses a primitive representative for the specified quadratic norm classes and gives a fixed tile for every sufficiently large multiplier. For a reduced coefficient `d`, the explicit conductor is smaller than `2d`. Its negative W-only-sector conclusion retains all residue and factor-of-3 restrictions. A strip recipe's exact cutoff is not a necessary cutoff for arbitrary tilings.

The two original package manifests were checked before import; all source-package files were preserved byte-for-byte. [Archive hashes](../../verification/october-imports.json) identify the inputs. Root CI now invokes both complete finite suites in disposable copies. This extends the verification coverage without presenting regression tests as proofs of the universal mathematics.

## Attempts directed at full classification

The goal of the second stage was an all-N converse, not another count search. The approaches examined were:

1. **Combine necessary spectra with eventual constructions.** This still leaves unbounded primitive shapes and small multipliers. No common finite exception list follows. Norm solvability supplies arithmetic representatives, not the missing small tilings.
2. **Compress every tiling into a bounded number of grid blocks.** The later archive already refutes the proposed absolute bound for these two block types. Reusing the older conjecture would contradict the project's own evidence.
3. **Strengthen boundary invariants, including ordered group labels and local congruences.** The saved formal witnesses and three-scale group lemma explain why these particular position-free tests accept exceptional small scales. No implication from those equalities to a positive planar dissection was established.
4. **Transfer the rectangle/electrical-network approach.** Effective-response reductions in rectangle tilings do not preserve the common congruent triangular tile or its exact count. The required transfer theorem was not obtained. Similarly, the elliptic-curve method used in problem 633 controls arithmetic possibilities rather than the remaining positive realization step here.
5. **Use the actual planar adjacency or flat gluing structure.** This retains the missing geometric information, but no uniform description of all realizable counts follows from the current local angle inventories. Enumerating all fixed-N maps would return to a decision procedure already known to be insufficient as the requested structural answer.

The stronger conjectural W/beta threshold `t≥v` was considered only as a research question. It was not proved or used for any negative conclusion.

## Precise endpoint

For the union of all classical and thirteen nonclassical branches, the investigation still lacks a necessary-and-sufficient description of geometric realizability at all remaining scales. A proof restricted to W/beta scale one would not supply that description for the other branches. The new exact arithmetic domain and constructive tails do not exhaust those remaining cases.

No complete solution, new prime classification, newly excluded N, external referee approval or priority claim is made. No private correspondence is included or sent by this integration.
