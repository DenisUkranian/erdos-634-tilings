## Harries's fixed-tile multiplier windows

The live [Harries manuscript](https://github.com/jphme/math-problems/blob/main/progress634/progress634.tex)
was fetched through GitHub. Its blob is still
`85536aabe013ad393e33985cbfcadbae50722f08`, dated 28 August 2026: the
same snapshot identified in the [30 September audit](../literature-audit-2026-09-30.md).

The precise source labels are `prop:addition` (multiplier addition),
`prop:imp-zhang11` (the imported Zhang Proposition 11 transfer), and
`cor:windows` (finite generator windows). Harries's row II is our F3.
For each fixed primitive tile, put

    R = ceil(a/b)+ceil(b/a),    C = ceil(3R/2).

Every F3 multiplier at least `C` is constructible, and its multiplier
semigroup is generated by its realizable elements in `[1,2C−1]`.

| Tile | F3 coefficient | Sufficient tail | Generator window |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `(8,7,13)` | 990 | `m≥5` | `1,…,9` |
| `(24,11,31)` | 4830 | `m≥6` | `1,…,11` |

These are prior sufficient bounds, not necessary thresholds. A finite
window for each fixed tile does not give a finite global seed list over
all tiles. The manuscript contains no separate mention of 990 or 4830.
Its broad prime-classification discussion is not used here; the manuscript
itself separates that discussion from its composite-count arguments.

## Attribution correction for the F3 vertex note

Beeson–Zhang's Table 2 gives the vertex inventory. In the proof of Lemma
3.5, Table 3 gives `C−S=2S₂+1`. With
`C=x`, `S=z+q+s`, and `S₂=w`, this is exactly the identity
`x=1+z+2w+q+s` reproduced in [the F3 note](../f3-vertex-defects.md).
The proof of Lemma 3.4 already establishes a c-edge on each external side.
Both statements are now explicitly credited there. The note separately
records its mismatch-injectivity and unit-atom consequences without
claiming priority or deciding the primitive candidates.
