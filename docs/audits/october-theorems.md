**Finding:** Theorem A and its enlarged-domain variant B have a complete necessity-and-sufficiency argument conditional on the declared exhaustive shape/rationality classification and the thirteen necessary integer-scale spectra. No proposed scale-one W/beta exclusion or all-primes candidate enters the proof.

1. For `N = 6 (mod 16)` with `3` not dividing `N`, the full residue calculation leaves only Group-1 alpha-isosceles and other-scalene QP. Every multiplier is odd, and multiplying a coefficient of 2-adic valuation one by an odd square preserves its residue modulo 16. Both difference-of-squares rows and every 60/120-degree row except the explicitly 3-divisible F3 row are eliminated.
2. In alpha, `b = 5 (mod 8)` and `Q/2 = 7 (mod 8)`. The coprime factors `b,Q` partition the odd squarefree kernel: `b=B s^2`, `Q=2A r^2`. Odd squares are 1 modulo 8. Every odd prime dividing primitive `Q=2v^2-u^2` splits in the quadratic field of 2. If no prime 7 modulo 8 has odd valuation in N, every prime of A is 1 modulo 8, contradicting `A=7 (mod 8)`. This argument correctly allows primes 7 modulo 8 with even exponent. The further Legendre conditions in B follow by reduction modulo primes in A or B, with all denominators nonzero because the factors are coprime.
3. Once alpha is removed, QP is necessary. Conversely the previously proved five-block QP construction tiles every primitive pair and every positive integer scale. This construction is visibly geometric, not merely an area identity. The factor criterion is equivalent to `v^2=P-Q`, `u^2=2P-3Q`; the strict factor inequalities give `0<u<v`, and `gcd(Q,P)=1` forces `gcd(u,v)=1`.
4. Theorem C follows from the same residue collapse and inert-prime tests. Its restrictions on factors 2 and 3 must remain explicit.

The package's asymptotic concentration theorem D is also supported by the written count: nine rows have O(sqrt(Y)) primitive coefficient parameters and hence O(sqrt(X) log X) square-multiple counts; the other three norm envelopes have density zero by divergent inert-prime reciprocal sums. Its remaining two-row arithmetic envelope has the stated complement and density. This theorem is weaker than the separate density-zero manuscript and does not rely on that manuscript's geometric seam lemma.

## Constructive square-class saturation

**Finding:** The constructive tail theorem has a complete uniform proof. The exact W-only-sector negative implication additionally uses the same published classification and necessary spectra as above. The positive theorem itself uses explicit geometric coordinates, integer grids, and elementary quadratic norm arithmetic.

1. For the established apex annulus the residual length is `L=ubT-a^2-b^2`. In a nonnegative representation `L=xb+yc`, reducing modulo b forces the least permissible coefficient `y0=b ceil(c/b)-c`; substitution gives `x0=uT-b-c(ceil(c/b)-1)`. Every other nonnegative y decreases x. Thus the stated H is the exact threshold for this particular mixed-strip recipe, not an arbitrary-tiling necessity.
2. The seed at each positive multiple of v and the external annulus adding u are actual dissections. For `m >= C = v ceil(H/v)+(u-1)(v-1)`, choose `0<=i<v` with `iu=m (mod v)`. Then `m-v ceil(H/v)-iu` is divisible by v and at least `-(v-1)`, hence nonnegative. Starting from that larger v-multiple and adding at most v-1 annuli constructs m. The proof never presumes a decomposition of the old tiling's interior.
3. The inequality `C<2Q<2P` is valid. For u=1, H=Q. For u>=2, `ub-c >= u^2-u-1 >0`, giving H<=Q; then `C <= Q+uv-u <2Q`.
4. The norm criteria and cone reduction were independently reconstructed. The rings Z[sqrt(2)] and Z[sqrt(3)] are norm Euclidean by rounding both rational coordinates. For sqrt(3), the sign restrictions modulo 3 are necessary and correctly retained; for sqrt(2), the norm-minus-one unit permits sign reversal. Reduction by the positive norm-one unit puts the coordinates in `0<=u<=v`; precisely the explicitly excluded small kernels occur at the endpoints. Squarefreeness forces primitive coordinates. Hence every compatible nondegenerate kernel has the asserted finite reduced representative.
5. The W-only exact tail keeps `d=14 (mod 16)`, `3` not dividing d, and `gcd(m,6)=1` on its negative direction. Positives beyond the threshold do not require the latter multiplier restriction. No absence claim outside that domain follows.

## Publication recommendation and limits

Both packages are suitable to publish as internally checked partial research, preserving their scope statements and antecedent attribution. The exact QP sector is the stronger headline because it is a necessary-and-sufficient all-tile statement on an infinite explicit domain. The linear square-class tail is a useful complementary constructive result. Neither establishes the full all-integer characterization. Small-scale W/beta and other surviving geometric families remain genuinely unclassified here; no timeout, arithmetic candidate, or finite regression supplies that missing implication.
