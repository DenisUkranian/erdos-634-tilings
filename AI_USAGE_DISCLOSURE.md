# Human direction and generative-AI assistance

Denis Paliy set the research direction, selected the research objectives and guided the investigation. ChatGPT assisted with exploring proof strategies, developing and revising arguments, writing checking programs, carrying out internal checks and preparing the presentation.

Denis Paliy is the named human author and maintainer. ChatGPT is not listed as an author. This disclosure records both the human direction and the software assistance involved in the work.

Mathematical correctness must be assessed from the supplied arguments, source code and exact certificates. Agreement between AI-assisted checking attempts is not external referee approval. Separately developed implementations within this project provide computational cross-checks; they are not independent human reviews.

The prime-case result is presented as a **candidate proof** requiring external scrutiny. The explicit 322-tile construction has a different evidentiary status: it is accompanied by exact coordinates and a separate geometric checker. Its general construction theorem has a written mathematical proof. None of these statements claims a full solution of Erdős problem 634, proof-assistant formalization or adjudicated priority.

The named human author retains responsibility for the material presented under his name. Readers are invited to inspect, reproduce and correct it through the public issue tracker. Future revisions should record any discovered gap or correction and preserve the distinction between finite replay and universal mathematical proof.
